Hunza Valley

Turquoise Attabad Lake, the Passu Cones, and 700-year-old Baltit Fort — Pakistan’s legendary mountain valley of apricot orchards, Ismaili hospitality, and Karakoram light.

The Legendary Shangri-La

Hunza sits along the Karakoram Highway where terraced fields climb toward Rakaposhi and Ultar. From Karimabad’s rooftops to the electric blue of Attabad — born of a 2010 landslide — the valley rewards slow travel: forts and folk stories by day, clear mountain nights, and warm local hospitality.

Highlights of Hunza

  • Attabad Lake — Boat across turquoise water framed by sheer cliffs and the distant Passu cones — one of Pakistan’s most photographed landscapes.
  • Baltit & Altit Forts — Explore restored royal forts above Karimabad and Altit, with living museums, village walks, and views over the Hunza River.
  • Passu & Upper Hunza — Walk suspension bridges, photograph the Passu Cathedral, and continue toward Sost and the Khunjerab corridor when seasons allow.
  • Culture & Cuisine — Apricot harvests, Wakhi and Burushaski hospitality, handmade crafts, and quiet evenings in mountain guesthouses.

When to Visit

Spring: Blossoms and mild days. Summer: Full road access toward high passes. Autumn: Golden poplars and clear air. Winter: Quiet snowscapes for adventurous travelers.
