Skardu

Shangrila’s turquoise shores, the vast Deosai Plains, and the road toward Concordia — Baltistan’s capital is the gateway to lakes, high plateaus, and the world’s highest peaks.

Land of Lakes & Giants

Skardu sits where the Indus broadens beneath the Karakoram. Travelers come for Lower Kachura (Shangrila), Satpara’s calm water, jeep days onto Deosai, and the logistics hub for serious mountain journeys — yet the town itself offers forts, bazaars, and Balti culture worth lingering for.

Highlights of Skardu

  • Shangrila & Satpara — Day trips to Lower Kachura’s famous lake resort and the quieter beauty of Satpara — classic Skardu photography and picnic stops.
  • Deosai National Park — Cross the world’s second-highest plateau in summer for wildflowers, Sheosar Lake, and vast open sky (seasonal access).
  • Shigar & Khaplu — Heritage fort-hotels, apricot orchards, and valleys that pair culture with soft adventure.
  • Mountain Gateways — Base for Askole / Baltoro approaches, cold-desert landscapes, and multi-day jeep or trek programs for fit travelers.

When to Visit

May–October: Best for lakes, Deosai, and high-road travel. Winter: Dramatic cold-desert scenery for prepared visitors; some high routes close.
