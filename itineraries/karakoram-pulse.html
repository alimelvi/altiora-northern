North Pakistan · Full logistics

Karakoram Pulse

Islamabad → Hunza → Passu → Skardu → Deosai → fly out — every transfer, lake stop, and overnight spelled out so you know exactly how the trip moves.

9 days / 8 nights Moderate · Scenic Hunza · Skardu

Trip snapshot

A complete northern circuit with flight buffers built in. Domestic mountain flights (ISB–GIL / ISB–KDU) can delay — we plan hotels and drivers around that reality.

  • Start / EndIslamabad International (ISB) — meet & farewell
  • RouteISB → Gilgit/Hunza → Passu → Skardu → Deosai → ISB
  • StylePrivate vehicle, boutique/guesthouse stays, flexible photo stops
  • Best seasonApril–October (Deosai typically mid-June to September)

Day-by-day — landing to takeoff

  • Day 1

    Land Islamabad · Fly north · Hunza night

    Meals: — / L* / D Overnight: Karimabad or Altit (Hunza)

    Your trip starts the moment you clear immigration.

    • Landing: Meet-and-greet at Islamabad International (ISB) with name board; assist with SIM / cash if needed.
    • Transfer: Private vehicle to domestic terminal for the morning/midday flight to Gilgit (GIL) — or Skardu (KDU) if we are running the reverse routing that week.
    • If flight delays: Day-use hotel or city café hold near ISB; we rebook the next available mountain flight and keep your Hunza room.
    • Arrival Gilgit: Driver meets you airside/exit; scenic drive up the KKH into Hunza (approx. 2.5–3.5 hrs depending on stops).
    • Evening: Check-in, short orientation walk in Karimabad/Altit, welcome dinner, brief on tomorrow’s Attabad plan.
  • Day 2

    Attabad Lake · Baltit Fort · Valley light

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Hunza
    • Morning: Drive to Attabad; boat ride on the turquoise lake (weather permitting) with cliff and Passu-cone views.
    • Midday: Lakeside lunch or return toward Karimabad for a local restaurant.
    • Afternoon: Guided visit to Baltit Fort (or Altit Fort) — history, terraces, and rooftop photography.
    • Golden hour: Optional Eagle’s Nest / Duikar viewpoint for Rakaposhi light (season and cloud dependent).
    • Evening: Free time for cafés and crafts; dinner at hotel or recommended local spot.
  • Day 3

    Full Passu day — cones, bridge, glacier breeze

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Passu or return Hunza (your choice at booking)
    • Morning: Drive Upper Hunza to Passu; Passu Cones / Cathedral viewpoints with plenty of photo stops.
    • Walk: Hussaini or Passu suspension bridge crossing (optional — we skip if wind/comfort says no).
    • Afternoon: Short, easy walk toward glacier viewpoints near Passu (light footwear day — not a heavy trek).
    • Late day: Tea stop in Passu village; return to Karimabad or overnight in a Passu lodge for quieter night skies.
  • Day 4

    Hunza → Skardu transfer day

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Skardu
    • Option A (preferred when flights work): Morning drive to Gilgit → fly GIL–KDU or connect via ISB if needed; driver meets in Skardu.
    • Option B (road): Full scenic jeep/car day via the Indus corridor toward Skardu (long day — packed lunch, multiple photo/rest stops).
    • Arrival Skardu: Check-in; short orientation drive past the Indus and town bazaar if light remains.
    • Evening: Briefing for Shangrila / Satpara and Deosai weather window.
  • Day 5

    Shangrila (Lower Kachura) · Satpara · Skardu Fort view

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Skardu
    • Morning: Lower Kachura / Shangrila lakeside — classic turquoise frames, resort gardens, optional boat.
    • Midday: Picnic or lakeside lunch.
    • Afternoon: Satpara Lake for quieter cold-desert shoreline; Kharpocho Fort viewpoint over Skardu if energy allows.
    • Evening: Free time; gear check if Deosai is confirmed for tomorrow (layers, sunscreen, snacks).
  • Day 6

    Deosai National Park day (or Shigar swap)

    Meals: B / packed L / D Overnight: Skardu
    • If Deosai open (typically mid-summer): Early departure by 4x4; plateau drive, wildflower season (timing varies), Sheosar Lake stop, packed lunch on the plains; return to Skardu by evening.
    • If Deosai closed / weather blocks: Full Shigar Valley day — Shigar Fort visit, village walk, apricot orchards in season, riverside lunch; same overnight in Skardu.
    • We confirm the call the night before based on road reports.
  • Day 7

    Shigar or Khaplu soft culture day

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Skardu (or heritage fort-hotel if upgraded)
    • Morning: Drive to Shigar or Khaplu (your preference at booking).
    • Day: Fort/palace visit, village lanes, slow photography, optional short riverside walk.
    • Evening: Return Skardu or overnight in a heritage property if you chose the upgrade.
  • Day 8

    Buffer / free frames · Prep for exit

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Skardu
    • Purpose: Absorb flight delays, weather, or a second lake morning.
    • Options: Extra Satpara sunrise, bazaar time, spa/rest at hotel, or a short local hike with the guide.
    • Evening: Confirm tomorrow’s KDU–ISB flight status; pack and hand carry-on mountain layers for cabin cold.
  • Day 9

    Skardu → Islamabad · Takeoff / onward

    Meals: B / L* Overnight: — (trip ends)
    • Morning: Hotel checkout; private transfer to Skardu Airport (KDU) with flight assist.
    • Fly: KDU → ISB (weather permitting). If cancelled, we hold you in Skardu and rebook — Day 8 exists for this.
    • Islamabad: Meet on arrival; transfer to ISB international terminal for your outbound flight, or to a day room / city hotel if your international departure is later.
    • Takeoff: Farewell at drop-off; WhatsApp support until you are airside.
