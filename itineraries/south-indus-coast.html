South & Heartland

Indus, Shrines & the Edge

City heat, 4,000-year-old streets, drum-filled shrine nights, and Mughal grandeur — a southern arc with texture, music, and stories you won’t get from the mountains alone.

9 days / 8 nights Culture · Road trip Sindh · Punjab

Trip snapshot

A full Indus-to-Lahore culture circuit with hotel check-ins, site entries, and road hours spelled out. Heat, traffic, and shrine etiquette shape the pace — we plan meals and transfers around that reality.

  • Start / EndKarachi (KHI) landing → Lahore (LHE) takeoff (or return KHI)
  • RouteKarachi → (optional Makran/Hingol) → Mohenjo-daro → Sehwan → Multan → Lahore
  • StylePrivate AC vehicle, city/heritage hotels, guided site days
  • Best seasonOctober–March (cooler road days; May–August we shift early starts)

Day-by-day — landing to takeoff

  • Day 1

    Land Karachi (KHI) · Hotel check-in · Soft city start

    Meals: — / L* / D Overnight: Karachi (Clifton / Defence / city hotel)

    Your trip starts the moment you clear immigration at Jinnah International.

    • Landing: Meet-and-greet at Karachi (KHI) with name board; assist with SIM / cash / hotel drop.
    • Transfer: Private AC vehicle to hotel (typically 45–90 min depending on traffic and terminal).
    • Afternoon: Check-in and rest; optional short orientation drive past Sea View / Clifton if energy allows.
    • Evening: Welcome dinner briefing — tomorrow’s Frere Hall / Manora plan, dress notes for later shrine days, and optional Makran flag if you booked the extension.
  • Day 2

    Karachi layers — Frere Hall · coast · museum option

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Karachi
    • Morning: Frere Hall gardens and colonial façade photography; drive through Saddar heritage pockets.
    • Midday: Lunch at a vetted city restaurant; optional Mohatta Palace or National Museum slot (confirm openings at booking).
    • Afternoon: Manora Island ferry / Clifton beach stretch — sea breeze, lighthouse frames, and fishing-harbour energy (tide and ferry schedule dependent).
    • Evening: Free time for cafés; dinner; pack for inland departure tomorrow.
  • Day 3

    Toward the Indus · Larkana corridor (Makran note)

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Larkana / Sukkur corridor hotel
    • Standard route: Early hotel checkout; private drive inland toward Larkana / Mohenjo-daro staging (approx. 7–9 hrs with meal and rest stops — packed or roadside lunch).
    • Optional Makran / Hingol: If pre-booked and roads/season allow, a coastal/Hingol National Park detour replaces or extends this transfer — we confirm feasibility at booking and may add a night.
    • Arrival: Check-in; short walk or tea stop; briefing for tomorrow’s archaeological day (sun, footwear, water).
    • Evening: Dinner at hotel; early night for site opening.
  • Day 4

    Mohenjo-daro — Great Bath · grid city · museum

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Larkana corridor or move toward Sehwan (as quoted)
    • Morning: Drive to the UNESCO site; guided walk of the Great Bath, lower town grid streets, citadel / stupa mound, and drainage systems with a site specialist.
    • Midday: Shade break and lunch; on-site museum for seals, pottery, and Indus context.
    • Afternoon: Second site loop for photography in softer light, or begin transfer toward Sehwan if overnight is staged there.
    • Evening: Hotel dinner; preview shrine etiquette for Sehwan Sharif.
  • Day 5

    Sehwan Sharif — shrine atmosphere & Sufi pulse

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Sehwan / Dadu corridor
    • Morning: Drive to Sehwan (timing depends on prior night’s base — typically 2–4 hrs).
    • Day: Visit the shrine of Lal Shahbaz Qalandar — courtyard, dhol energy (especially evenings / Thursday rhythm), and living Sufi tradition.
    • Etiquette: Modest dress covering arms/legs; remove shoes where required; ask before photographing devotees; follow your host on where to stand during rituals.
    • Evening: Optional shrine-side time if the atmosphere is active; dinner; rest for Multan transfer.
  • Day 6

    Sehwan → Multan — City of Saints

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Multan
    • Morning: Road transfer Sehwan → Multan (approx. 5–7 hrs with stops); packed or restaurant lunch en route.
    • Afternoon: Check-in; visit Shah Rukn-e-Alam tomb complex — blue-tile silhouette, brickwork, and courtyard photography.
    • Late day: Optional second Multani tomb / bazaar walk for Multani craft and seasonal mango sweets.
    • Evening: Dinner; brief on Lahore link options (road vs short flight).
  • Day 7

    Multan → Lahore · Fort–mosque skyline if light remains

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Lahore
    • Option A (road): Full Multan → Lahore drive (approx. 5–6.5 hrs); meal stop mid-way; arrival and hotel check-in.
    • Option B (air): MUX → LHE flight when schedules suit; driver meets at Allama Iqbal International (LHE).
    • If arrival allows: Soft evening walk toward Badshahi / Lahore Fort outer views and Food Street energy — not a full interior tour yet.
    • Evening: Dinner; tomorrow’s Mughal day order (fort first or mosque first based on ticket timing).
  • Day 8

    Mughal Lahore — Badshahi Mosque · Lahore Fort · old city

    Meals: B / L / D Overnight: Lahore
    • Morning: Badshahi Mosque — courtyard, minarets, and respectful photography (non-prayer windows preferred).
    • Midday: Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila) — Sheesh Mahal / picture wall highlights as open; lunch in the old-city / Food Street zone.
    • Afternoon: Walled-city lanes, Haveli façades, and snack stops; optional Wazir Khan Mosque if time and energy allow.
    • Evening: Free time for markets; farewell dinner; confirm tomorrow’s LHE (or KHI return) flight.
  • Day 9

    Lahore buffer hour · Airport farewell (LHE or KHI)

    Meals: B / L* Overnight: — (trip ends)
    • Morning: Hotel checkout; optional short Data Darbar exterior visit or last market hour if departure is late.
    • Standard exit: Private transfer to Lahore Airport (LHE) with flight assist — allow 60–90 min city-to-terminal in traffic.
    • Alternate: If you return via Karachi, LHE → KHI domestic then international, or full road/air combo as booked.
    • Takeoff: Farewell at drop-off; WhatsApp support until you are airside.
