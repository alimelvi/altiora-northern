FAQ

Common Questions

Do you only operate in northern Pakistan?

No. The north is one of our strengths, but we operate nationwide — Indus Valley sites, Sufi circuits, Gandhara, Mughal cities, Hindu temples, Victorian heritage, and adventure routes across the country.

Can you combine culture and adventure in one trip?

Yes. Many guests combine Lahore or Taxila with Hunza or Fairy Meadows. We design hybrid itineraries that respect travel time and season.

What are the best Pakistan tour packages for first-time visitors?

Most first-timers love a northern circuit (Hunza and Skardu) with Lahore. Start with our Karakoram Pulse itinerary; add south heritage if you have 14+ days.

When is the best time to visit northern Pakistan?

April to October is the main window for Hunza and Skardu road access and clear views. Autumn brings golden poplars; spring brings blossoms. We refine dates around flights and high passes.

Do you work with international travel companies?

Yes. We provide full DMC ground handling — itineraries, hotels, transport, guides, permits, and 24/7 support for agencies worldwide.
